Why join bhs87.com?

bhs87.com is the Balboa High School Class of '87's own site for our 40th reunion at the Canal Zone Reunion in Panama City, Panama, January 17–24, 2027. Tell the class when you'll be around, and see who else is coming and what they want to do.

What you get when you sign up

Your reunion week, planned in minutes

Enter your dates in Panama, answer for the Yacht Club '87 Dinner/Dance, and tick the CZR and MiEvento events you plan to attend or already have tickets for. They go straight onto your calendar, with time clashes flagged.

See who's in town, and when

The group dashboard shows which classmates are in Panama on which days, a heatmap of when most of the class is free, and the best mornings, afternoons and evenings to get together.

Find classmates who want the same things

Pick or suggest outings, from a Napoli lunch to a day at Coronado Beach or a jog on the Amador Causeway. When enough classmates share one, it becomes an interest group with the best times, venue ideas and a group chat link.

Know before tickets sell out

Ticket Watch shows which Canal Zone Reunion events on MiEvento are sold out or almost gone, refreshed through the season, with a direct link to buy.

Not in the yearbook? Add your photo

Coming to the reunion, but your photo isn't in the '87 yearbook? Here's your chance: add a photo to the Missing Photos page so the class can put a face to your name.

Lead an outing, if you'd like

Volunteer as an Event Organizer for an interest group, post the plan and the chat link, and the classmates who signed up for it see it on the dashboard.

How your sign-up helps the whole class

Better times for everyone

Every entry sharpens the group picture. The more classmates who mark their days, the more reliable the "best windows" become for dinners, tours and get-togethers.

Real numbers for organizers

Organizers can book tables, boats and outings with real head-counts instead of guesses.

No one gets missed

The more classmates who claim their names, the easier it is to spot who still hasn't heard about the reunion and pass the word along. The yearbook also remembers classmates we've lost.

A class home that lasts

bhs87.com stays up after January as the Class of '87's place online, with the roster, the yearbook and the photos all in one spot.

How it works

  1. Find your name. Type part of your yearbook or current name. Not on the list? Add it; an organizer confirms it.
  2. Confirm your email. Click the one-time link we send. That links the email to your name. No password to remember.
  3. Add your dates and events. Arrival and departure, the Yacht Club '87 Dinner/Dance, and the CZR events you're going to.
  4. Mark your free time and interests. Available, Maybe or busy for each morning, afternoon and evening, and the outings you'd join.
  5. Check the Group dashboard. See who's around, the best times to meet, and the interest groups forming. Come back and update any time.

Questions

Who can sign up?

Members of the Balboa High School Class of 1987 (Balboa, former Panama Canal Zone), including Honorary '87 classmates who were part of the class but didn't graduate with it. Find your name on the class roster; if it isn't there, add it and an organizer will confirm it.

Does it cost anything?

bhs87.com is free. However, tickets for Canal Zone Reunion events are bought through MiEvento, and the BHS '87 class evening Dinner/Dance must be paid to the class organizer (sign in to see the Zelle payment info).

Do I need a password?

No. Pick your name and enter your email; the site sends a one-time sign-in link. Clicking it links that email to your name, so nobody else can claim or change your entry.

What will classmates see about me?

Your name, the dates you'll be in Panama, and the outings you're interested in show on the group dashboard so classmates can plan around them. Your email address is never shown on the site.

When and where is the reunion?

The Canal Zone Reunion (CZR) runs January 17–24, 2027 in Panama City, Panama. The class's Yacht Club '87 Dinner/Dance is the evening of Wednesday, January 20, 2027. bhs87.com plans the wider window of January 9–30, 2027 for classmates arriving early or staying on.

I'm not sure I can go. Should I still sign up?

Yes. Claiming your name keeps you on the class roster and in the yearbook, lets you add a current photo, and you can mark "can't make it" for any event. You can change your answers any time.
